## Discovery

- **Section Label:** Discovery
- **Headline:** A feed that gets it.
- **Subhead:** Vitrine learns what you collect and shows you more of it. No algorithm guessing. No irrelevant noise. Just the stuff you actually want to see.

### Discovery Feed Mock UI Elements

- Activity ticker: rdvault listed PSA 10 Charizard — +142 collectors

- Main card: 1999 Charizard Holo — 48m ago — $42,500 — +12.4% — CardVault — 1.8K tracking

### Feature Details

**Calibrated to you.**
When you sign up, you tell us what you collect. From there, the feed learns—what you view, what you track, what you admire. It gets sharper over time.

**Good from day one.**
Most apps show you garbage until they figure you out. Vitrine asks the right questions upfront so your feed is useful immediately—not after two weeks of training it.

**Multiple ways in.**

| Feed | Description |
|------|-------------|
| For You | Personalized recommendations based on your behavior |
| Trending | What's getting attention across the platform |
| Browse by Category | Dive into a specific collecting world |

## Messaging

- **Section Label:** Messaging
- **Headline:** Your collection is part of the conversation.
- **Subhead:** Vitrine messaging isn't just chat bolted on. It's built around the things you collect.

### Chat Mock UI Elements

- Chat header: Marcus Chen — @marcusgrails
- Received: Hey, saw your Grail Collection. That Chicago 1 is incredible.
- Sent: Thanks! Been holding it since 2019. Here's the listing:
  - Embedded collectible: Air Jordan 1 High OG — "Chicago" 1985 — $12,500
- Received: Wow. Would you consider a trade + cash?
- Attachment sheet options: Photo (From camera or gallery), Collectible (Share from your collection), Showcase (Share a showcase)

### Feature Details

**See something? Say something.**
Every listing has a direct line to the collector who owns it. No hunting for a username. No jumping to another app. Tap, message, done.

**Drop items into the chat. Literally.**
Attach any collectible or Showcase directly to a message. Not a screenshot. Not a link. The actual item from your catalog, rendered inline. This is how collectors should talk.

**One place for all of it.**
Your collection, your conversations, your community—all in the same app. No more "DM me on Instagram" or "check my bio for contact."

### Section Closing

- The item *is* the conversation starter. We just made it official.
